## The problem

CityFix connects citizens, service teams, and a municipal Command Center. Its CityCycle waste-and-recycling component links household pickup requests with collectors, material weights, recorded payments, and smart-bin readings.

The project won first place at the SolveThe17 2.0 Hackathon hosted by the IEEE EUI Student Branch.

## My contribution

I contributed to ideation, the mobile application, frontend development, smart-bin prototyping, and integration across the team platform. I developed citizen and collector interfaces with React Native, Expo, and TypeScript and connected those workflows with the wider system.

## Workflow and architecture

A household requests a recycling pickup. A collector accepts the job, records material weights and the amount paid, and the household confirms the weight. Mobile, citizen-web, and Command Center clients share a FastAPI backend. Smart-bin simulators submit fill readings for monitoring and route planning.

The payment records track amounts; the platform does not move money or integrate a payment provider.

## Prototype status

The repository includes backend tests for pickups, bins, route optimization, and fill forecasting. Citizen-web and mobile interfaces use mock mode by default. Hardware consists of browser and PC simulators, ESP32 Wokwi prototypes, and design documents; it has not been validated as a physical build.

Team-provided hosted URLs are documented in the repository. Live end-to-end operation and production OR-Tools behavior remain unverified.
